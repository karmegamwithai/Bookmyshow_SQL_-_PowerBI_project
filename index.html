Project overview

Business problem

BookMyShow data is distributed across customers, movies, theaters, screens, shows, bookings, seats, payments and reviews. The business needs a consistent analytical view to monitor demand, revenue, theater utilization, transaction performance and customer satisfaction.

Business objectives

  1. Analyze customer registration, booking behavior and repeat customers.
  2. Identify popular movies, genres and languages.
  3. Evaluate theater and city-wise revenue performance.
  4. Monitor ticket sales, show demand and seat occupancy.
  5. Analyze monthly booking and revenue trends.
  6. Evaluate payment success/failure and customer satisfaction.

Technology stack

MySQL 8+Power BIExcel

Excel supports data validation and preliminary exploration. MySQL handles relational analysis; Power BI delivers interactive reporting.

Key business KPIs

  • Total users, movies, theaters and bookings
  • Tickets sold and total recognized revenue
  • Average ticket price and movie review rating
  • Repeat customers and payment success rate
  • Seat occupancy percentage and revenue per booking

Expected business outcomes

  • Identify popular movies and genres.
  • Understand repeat customer patterns.
  • Recognize top revenue-generating cities and theaters.
  • Improve show scheduling using ticket demand.
  • Monitor payment failures and customer satisfaction.
